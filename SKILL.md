If the user invokes this skill without any other guidance, ask them what they want to build or design, ask some questions, and act as an expert designer who outputs HTML artifacts _or_ production code, depending on the need.

Key rules (details in readme.md):
- Navy-deep canvas, navy-raised cards, bronze hairlines, gold for selection/focus, ivory text. Crimson is alert/brand accent — never body text, never the automatic "wrong answer" colour (use the ember/patina/ash state tokens with icon + text).
- Cinzel caps for brand/ceremonial titles, Cormorant italic for subtitles/motto, Georgia for content headings, Inter for all study reading and UI, JetBrains Mono for codes/timers/legal refs.
- Cinematic scenes (citadel day/night/sunset) only on welcome, dashboard banner and milestones — behind a navy protection gradient. Questions, flashcards, mind maps and reviews stay plain and quiet.
- Brand symbols (Soldado, Sábio, Estrategista, Ascensão, sword marks) are supplied images in `assets/` — never redraw, recolor or replace with emoji. UI glyphs are Lucide outline icons.
- AI tutor and spaced repetition must be honestly labelled "Demonstração" until real.
